# Product direction

Working site name: Shop Lab (may change).

## Product

- Shop Lab teaches system design to engineers who have no background in it.
- It uses everyday experience and mini games, not lectures.
- The reader should leave able to explain each concept in plain words.

## World

- The setting is a small food shop that keeps growing.
- Each concept is a new problem the shop meets as it grows.
- Tie each lesson back to the shop where it fits. A concept with no natural shop story can still be taught.

## Lesson rhythm

1. Play first. The learner runs the shop in a mini game.
2. Feel the problem. Let the shop struggle before any explanation.
3. Reveal the engineering term. Name the concept only after the learner has felt it.

Do not show the term before the play step.

## Game style

- Use management sim mechanics: resources, queues, upgrades, trade-offs.
- Use original names, art and characters only.
- Do not copy the look, names or characters of any existing game.

## Content rules

- Write everything in our own words.
- Do not copy source text or images.
- Ship in two languages: English and Traditional Chinese.
- Each concept has one agreed term in each language. Use that term every time.
- Keep the term list with the content in `src/content/`.
- Keep sentences short and plain.

## Non-goals

- This is not an interview question bank.
- It does not cover every technical detail. Teach the core idea, then stop.
- Concept lists and lesson text do not belong in this file. They live in `src/content/`.
